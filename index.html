The Ranch

Outfitting Trophy Hunts in Southern Oklahoma since 2005

Buckhorn Ranch sits on 1,700 high-fenced acres in the Arbuckle Mountains outside Sulphur, Oklahoma. 

From the moment you arrive at the gate, we handle everything — morning calls, field prep, meals cooked on site, and trophies scored and packaged for shipment.

The land has been in the family for decades. The Arbuckle Mountains aren't flashy country — cedar thickets, limestone bluffs, and creek drainages that hold deer when everything else is pushed out. The ranch has been managed for hunting since the early 2000s, with the high-fence system going in after years of watching deer walk off the property during gun season.

The lodge came next. It was built to handle serious hunting parties, not weekend guests — walk-in cooler, skinning shed, rifle range on site, room for ten hunters and their gear. The kitchen has always been a priority. If you're up at 4:30 and back by noon, you eat well.

The exotic program started with a handful of animals and grew from there. Gemsbok, red stag, fallow deer, zebra, scimitar oryx — the herd is managed year-round. Whitetail season runs October through December. Exotics are available any time. The ranch hasn't had to advertise much. Most bookings come from people who have been here before, or from someone they brought.
